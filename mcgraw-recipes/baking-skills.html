Beginner baking skills

These are general skills that will improve baking technique. Below are skills that recipes assume you know.

  • Flour is not meant to be compacted. This means you should fluff up your flour with a knife, then scoop up the loose flour and brush off the remaining flour piled up at the top with a knife. This will ensure your flour is correctly measured.
  • Brown sugar is meant to be compacted. To do this pretend like you're four years old and you want to build a sand castle out of brown sugar. First scoop as much as you can, then compact it. It should come out of the measuring cup as a solid peice of sugar.
  • Over mixing usually isn't a problem. I will specifically mention if overmixing could cause issues.
  • Think about order of steps. When I bake I like to have a wet and dry ingredient bowl. I will often put the wet ingredients in a bigger bowl since it's easy to pour dry ingredients out of a bowl, but wet ingredients like to stick to the edges.
  • Preheating the oven should be the first step unless the dough needs to chill or sit for a time.
