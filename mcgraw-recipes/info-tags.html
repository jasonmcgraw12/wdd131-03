Difficulty

Difficulty is measured by how many tools you need to make the recipe, specific techniques, and multiple steps. Some specific things that increase difficulty are listed below.

  • Snickerdoodles use a lot of different sized measuring cups.
  • Pie dough requires rolling out dough which is difficult if you don't know how to do it.
  • Lemon squares require a crust and a filling. These multiple steps increase difficulty.

Time

Time is measured by active and passive time spent. Active time is measured by the ammount of time one person takes to make a recipe. Passive time is measured by things that don't require a person. Some examples of active and passive actions are listed below.

Active actions:

  • Mixing dough
  • Rolling dough
  • Decorating cookies

Passive actions:

  • chilling dough
  • Baking dough
  • Waiting for bread to rise

Yummyness

Yummyness is purely opinion based and is based on my own preferences. Below are things I take into acount when judging yummyness.

  • Texture
  • Taste
  • Does it make me feel sick if I eat a dozen?
