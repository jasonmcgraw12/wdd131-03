Site Plan

Purpose and Audience

I was thinking of making a website with recipies that my family and roomates could use. Until now I have bookmarked recipies that I use, but I think it would be easier for me to use a website with my favorite recipies instead of bookmarked tabs. Also the baking websites take a long time to load because of all the adds.

Description of the Dynamic Elements

I was thinking that there would be a search bar that would sort the recipies, and have checkboxes with ingredients to remove recipies if you don't have an ingredient (like if you didn't have eggs you could remove all recipies with eggs).

With the child page I was thinking it would be the same page for every recipe, but it would dynamically update depending on the recipe you chose.
